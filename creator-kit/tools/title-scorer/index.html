How the score works

The score is a checklist, not a prediction. It adds up seven simple rules, the same ones the Title & Thumbnail Lab in the Faceless Creator Kit uses, and shows you every point so you can decide for yourself:

RulePointsWhy it's there
Length 40–65 characters20Long titles get cut off in many places YouTube shows them; very short ones often say too little.
Has a number15"7 things" or "in 10 minutes" sets a clear expectation.
A curiosity or pain word15Words like mistake, hidden or why name the problem the video solves.
Strong start15"In this video…" wastes the first words; lead with the payoff.
At most 2 ALL CAPS words10One emphasized word can help; a shouting title looks like spam.
No clickbait words10"Shocking" and "you won't believe" promise more than most videos deliver.
Your keyword15The phrase people actually search for should be in the title.

A high score doesn't mean a video will do well, and a lower one isn't automatically bad: a story title without a number can be the right call. Use the reasons, not just the number.

How to use it in five minutes

  1. Write three different angles, not three versions of one sentence: a list ("7 Things…"), a mistake ("The Mistake That…") and a question or how-to.
  2. Add your keyword — the two or three words someone would type into YouTube search to find this video.
  3. Read the orange and red lines. Fix the cheap ones first: move the payoff to the front, cut filler words, add the number you already have in the video.
  4. Check it still sounds like a person wrote it. If a rewrite only exists to tick a box, keep the clearer original.
  5. Copy the best title and pair it with thumbnail text that adds something new — 2 to 5 words that don't repeat the title.

Tips for faceless channels

Without a face on the thumbnail, the title and the thumbnail text carry the whole promise. Be specific about the payoff ("Without a Telescope", "Before You Buy"), keep one idea per title, and make sure the first 30 seconds of the video deliver exactly what the title says. Viewers who feel tricked leave early, and that hurts more than a modest title ever will.
