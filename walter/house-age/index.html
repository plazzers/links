What to check in a house built in…

Why this matters

Every house carries the habits of the year it was built. Builders used what was normal at the time — the wiring, the pipes, the insulation — and we've learned a lot about some of those materials since.

None of this means your house is in trouble. Most of it is just worth knowing about, so you know where to look and what to ask. Pick the year and I'll show you what I'd look at first. — Walter
