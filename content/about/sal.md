**Chef Sal Romano** is a YouTube channel and a set of free guides about two things: how restaurants nudge you into spending more, and how to make the dishes you love at home for a fraction of the price. It's made for home cooks — no fancy equipment, no hard-to-find ingredients.

## What you'll find here

- **Restaurant secrets** — menu design, specials, drink prices, upsells, and how to order smarter when you do eat out.
- **Copycat dishes** — what makes a popular restaurant-style dish work and the techniques that matter, with time and rough cost at home. Three recipes are free in full: [Sunday Marinara](../guides/sunday-marinara-sauce/), [Garlic Butter Sauce](../guides/garlic-butter-sauce/) and [Buttermilk Ranch](../guides/buttermilk-ranch-dressing/).
- **A free calculator** — [Restaurant or Home?](../restaurant-or-home/) shows what you keep by cooking a meal yourself.
- **Videos** — on the [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@ChefSalRomano).

Opinions about food are exactly that — personal taste. We don't name restaurant chains in these guides, and we don't make health claims about anyone's food.

## How the products work

The full recipes, with exact amounts and step-by-step methods, are the paid products. They're digital and sold through Payhip, a checkout service for digital downloads:

- **Sal's 25 Rules for Eating Out** — a free PDF.
- **Sal's Restaurant Copycat Cookbook** — 33 restaurant-style dishes to make at home.
- **Sal's Kitchen App** — the same 33 dishes on your phone, with a shopping list and cooking timers.
- **Sal's Italian Kitchen** — 60 Italian recipes, from antipasti to dolci.

Links to Payhip carry a small tracking tag so we can see which guide was useful. There are no cookies, ads or trackers on these pages. Home costs in the guides are rough supermarket estimates; prices where you live will differ.

## The fine print

Recipes are inspired by popular restaurant dishes. Not affiliated with or endorsed by any restaurant. There are no reviews, ratings or customer quotes on this site, and we never make them up.
