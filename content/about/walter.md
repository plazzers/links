**Walter's Home Check** is a YouTube channel and a set of free guides about the parts of a house most people never look at — until they become a problem. The idea is simple: show you what to look at, explain why it matters in plain English, and be clear about when it's time to call a licensed pro.

## What you'll find here

- **Guides** — practical articles on buying a house, seasonal maintenance, home systems (roof, water heater, furnace, alarms) and the issues that come with older homes.
- **Free tools** — like [What to Check in a House Built in…](../house-age/), which turns the year your house was built into a short checklist.
- **Videos** — the same kind of walk-throughs on the [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@WaltersHomeCheck).

Every guide separates the things you can safely look at yourself from the things that need a qualified professional — an electrician, plumber, roofer, structural engineer, a qualified home inspector, or a radon, asbestos or lead specialist. Where a guide gives a specific fact, it lists the public source (EPA, CPSC, NFPA, the US Fire Administration, energy.gov and others) at the end.

## How the products work

Some guides mention paid products. They're digital and sold through Payhip, a checkout service for digital downloads:

- **The Weekend Home Check** — a free printable PDF: 25 things to check in about 30 minutes.
- **Walter's Home Check App** — the full room-by-room checklist on your phone, with photos, notes and a PDF report.
- **The Home Check Manual** — a room-by-room guide with a seasonal calendar and a home record page.
- **The House Buyer's Red Flag Checklist** — 50 things to check before you buy.

Links to Payhip carry a small tracking tag so we can see which guide was useful. There are no cookies, ads or trackers on these pages.

## The fine print

The guides are educational. They're written to help you notice things and ask better questions, not to diagnose your house. Not a substitute for a professional inspection of your property — when something looks wrong, or when safety is involved, call a licensed pro. We don't publish repair prices because they vary too much by area and by house.

There are no reviews, ratings or customer quotes on this site, and we never make them up.
